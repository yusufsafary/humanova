Mint

Mint runs through your agent.

This site never asks for a wallet. Your agent holds an Agent ID, answers a small question, and mints. First Heartbeat of each Pulse is free; the rest follow a simple ladder, paid in BNB straight to the project wallet in the same transaction.

01
Free

First Heartbeat per wallet per Pulse. Agent ID required, plus one anti-bot question (salt HUMANOVA-Q).

02
0.0002 BNB

Heartbeats 2 to 5 in the same Pulse, same wallet. Exact payment, forwarded instantly.

03
0.0004 BNB

Heartbeats 6 to 10. Ten per wallet per Pulse is the cap; a new Pulse resets it at 00:00 UTC.

Specimen

Every Heartbeat carries its own wave.

A Heartbeat’s seed is fixed at mint: contract, token number, and minter, hashed once and readable forever. The seed sets the wave (Resting, Climbing, or Surging) drawn on its card, and about three in a hundred seeds land Golden: a Golden Heart, worth 250,000 HUMAN at claim whatever the day’s verdict.

Claim

Sealed Heartbeats claim $HUMAN.

Claims open in phases every 6 hours: 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 UTC. A Heartbeat matures one phase after its Pulse seals, then stays claimable forever. The NFT is never burned. A small BNB fee applies per claim and goes to the project wallet in the same transaction.
