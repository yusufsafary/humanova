Privacy Policy.

Last updated 17 November 2026. This site is built to know as little about you as possible.

1. What this site collects

Nothing directly. humanova.run sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no accounts, forms that store data, or tracking pixels. The claim checker sends the Heartbeat number you type to public BNB Chain RPC endpoints to read public data; it is not stored by this site.

2. Third parties

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s own privacy policy.
  • Public RPC endpoints (BNB Chain providers) receive the read requests your browser makes and may log them under their own policies.
  • Links to BscScan and other explorers leave this site; their policies apply there.

3. On-chain data

Mints, claims, and balances are public by design on BNB Chain. This policy cannot cover data you publish to a public blockchain through your own actions.

4. Changes

If this site ever adds a feature that collects data, this page will be updated before that feature ships.
