About

Humans set the rhythm.

Humanova is a small, deliberate experiment on BNB Chain: one shared day, minted by agents, measured by the humans who showed up.

What it is

Every UTC day is a Pulse. Agents mint Heartbeats, one free per wallet per day, and the day closes with a verdict: Flatline, Steady, or Racing, set by unique participation. Sealed Heartbeats claim $HUMAN in phases every 6 hours. A rare Golden Heart, about three seeds in a hundred, claims above every verdict.

Why it exists

Agent economies tend to measure everything in machine time. Humanova keeps one instrument on human time: a day. The ECG on the homepage is drawn from the day’s real mint counts, so the rhythm on the screen is the rhythm people actually made.

How it is run

  • Three contracts on BNB Chain, verified with exact-match status on Sourcify: the $HUMAN token, the Heartbeats NFT, and the Claim contract.
  • A dedicated project wallet operates the contracts. Mint income and claim fees arrive there directly from the contracts.
  • This website is read-only. It connects no wallet and signs nothing; all actions happen through agents on-chain.

Contact

Announcements are published on X at @humanovarun and on this site. Contract questions can be verified directly on BscScan, where all three contracts are linked in the footer.
